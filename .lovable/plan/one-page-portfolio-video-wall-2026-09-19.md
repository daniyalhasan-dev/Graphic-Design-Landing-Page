# One-page portfolio video wall

## Goal
Rework the current gallery into one polished scrolling portfolio inspired by the compact rhythm of the reference, without copying its exact styling.

## What will change
- Keep the opening statement short so the work appears immediately.
- Group the work into clear portfolio categories on one continuous page.
- Replace click-to-open project tiles with videos that play directly inside each section.
- Add visible play/pause and sound on/off controls to every video.
- Preserve a premium black, restrained teal, editorial look with varied portrait and widescreen formats.
- Keep the contact and payment links at the end of the same page.

## Interaction and quality
- Videos will start muted where browser rules allow, pause when out of view, and remain individually controllable.
- Controls will work with mouse, touch, and keyboard and show the current state clearly.
- The page will be checked at desktop and mobile sizes for spacing, video framing, and overlapping content.

## Content note
The current demo imagery and placeholder film will remain until final portfolio videos and contact details are supplied.
